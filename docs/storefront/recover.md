# Recover a repomap Team licence

Current repomap capabilities and multi-repository workspaces are free and do
not need a licence. New Team sales and device claims are closed.

If you bought Team before it was retired, enter the email used at checkout. If
a paid legacy licence exists, Money sends an expiring recovery link. The
response never says whether that address has a purchase, and no token is shown
to an unverified browser.

Open the recovery link within one hour to view and copy your token. For a
legacy installation, run `repomap licence activate '<your token>'`; current
multi-repository workspaces need no activation. Do not share a licence token
or recovery link in a public issue, log or repository.
